🌐 Web3 Job Research Assignment

Find 5 fresh, direct job postings per category and post to the correct Telegram channel

🎯

Objective

Build a spreadsheet of fresh, relevant Web3/crypto job postings — one sheet per job category. For each category, find 5 current job listings, verify the direct company link, and post to the matching Telegram channel.

⚠️

Link Rules — Always Use the Best Available Link

The goal is to save job seekers clicks. Always check if "Apply" leads to a company website. If it does — use that. If it doesn't — the job board link is fine.

✅ BEST — Direct company link
ripple.com/careers/opening/123

Job board "Apply" button led here → use this link

✅ OK — Job board link
cryptojobslist.com/jobs/ripple-bd

"Apply" button stayed on the job board → this link is acceptable

❌ NEVER — Extra clicks for no reason
cryptojobslist.com/jobs/ripple-bd

You found the direct company link but posted the job board link anyway

Decision flow:
1. Find job on any job board
2. Click "Apply" or "View Job"
3. Did it open the company's own website?
   → Yes — copy that company URL and use it
   → No (stayed on job board) — the job board link is fine to use

Quality Checklist — Before Submitting Each Job

  • Link goes directly to the company's own job posting (not a job board)
  • Job was posted within the last 1–2 weeks (check the date on the listing)
  • Role clearly matches the category of the channel
  • No duplicate companies within the same sheet (5 different companies)
  • Job is in Web3 / Crypto / Blockchain industry (not traditional finance or tech)
  • Link actually opens and works — not expired or 404
